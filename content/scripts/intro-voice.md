## Recording tips
- Quiet room, phone 20-30 cm from your mouth, no music in the background.
- Calm pace, a short pause after each line (about half a second).
- Read "800" as "osiemset", "DM" as "di-em".
- 2-3 takes; send the best one (voice memo or .m4a).
