# Setup Knowledge
Ask the user in short batches (skip what they already gave; read their website if they share it):
1. Business, product, prices, booking & payment links -> knowledge/offer.md
2. Audience, ICP, pains -> knowledge/audience.md (then have audience-researcher enrich it)
3. Voice & visual style, examples they love -> knowledge/voice.md
4. Channels, handles, goals, volume -> knowledge/channels.md
5. What agents may do alone vs. must escalate -> knowledge/rules.md
6. Industry restrictions -> knowledge/compliance.md
7. North-star metric and targets -> knowledge/reporting.md
8. Content pillars -> knowledge/content.md
Leave [TBD] for unknowns. Finish with a summary of gaps.
