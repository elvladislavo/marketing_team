# Social Posts

For each requested platform produce:
- **Hook** (first line that stops the scroll)
- **Post copy** following platform norms (X <280 chars or a thread; LinkedIn short paragraphs; Instagram caption + hashtags; TikTok/Reels 15-60s script with shot list)
- **Visual direction** + alt text
- **CTA** and suggested posting time
- 2 alternative hooks for testing

Follow `knowledge/voice.md`. Save to `content/drafts/<topic-slug>.md`.
