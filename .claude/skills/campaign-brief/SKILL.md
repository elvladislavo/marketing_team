# Campaign Brief

Gather (ask only for what's missing): product/offer, business goal, audience, budget, deadline.

Produce `content/strategy/<campaign-slug>-brief.md` with:
1. **Background** - why now
2. **Objective** - SMART goal
3. **Target audience** - persona, pains, objections
4. **Key message** - one sentence + 3 supporting proof points
5. **Offer & CTA**
6. **Channels** - role of each channel and owner agent (see orchestrator team table)
7. **Deliverables list** - asset, owner, due date
8. **Timeline** - phases (pre-launch, launch, sustain)
9. **Budget split**
10. **KPIs & measurement** - primary/secondary metrics, tracking (UTMs)
11. **Risks & assumptions**
