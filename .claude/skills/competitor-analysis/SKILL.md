# Competitor Analysis

1. Confirm the list of competitors (ask or find via WebSearch).
2. For each, research website, pricing, messaging, social presence, content/SEO, reviews (G2, Capterra, app stores).
3. Output `deliverables/strategy/competitor-analysis.md`:
   - Comparison table: target audience, positioning, key message, pricing, top channels, strengths, weaknesses
   - Messaging map (what each claims to own)
   - SWOT for our brand
   - White-space opportunities and recommended differentiators
   - Sales battlecard per competitor: "when they say X, we say Y"
Cite sources with URLs; mark unverified info.
